Our edge

04 · Owning The Night

We don't tell them to drink 360. We own the room where they do.

Meet & Greet
Creator and tastemaker nights that pull a crowd and put Cherry in hand.
Speakeasy
Intimate after-dark rooms where the brand is the energy of the moment.
Speed Dating
Powered by 360 — Cherry as the hero of the night's most social hour.
The in-venue mixer mechanic
At a point in the night, 360 Cherry is served as a mixer — with rum, with vodka. Not 360's message: it runs through venue partnerships in Cap-Haïtien, Les Gonaïves and Limonade — bar owners, servers and the menu present it: “360 Energy Cherry + rum / vodka,” spirit brand unnamed.
Responsible by design
Kept out of 360's paid and social communication entirely. The mixer association lives only inside the venue — owning the occasion while protecting the brand.
