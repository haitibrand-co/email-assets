The idea

01Everything you do, you do it at 360

360 is not a moment in the day. 360 is the whole circle of it. The degree sign is the promise: we are in every part of your life — at sunrise when the work begins, and after dark when the living begins.

One human truth carries the brand: energy lived with honor by day, and with passion by night. Two faces of the same idea — and two audiences who together close the circle. That is 360 Live: you live your life fully, boldly, on your own terms — and 360 is the energy that lets you.

Tout sa w ap fè, ou fè l a 360. — Everything you do, you do it at 360.
The competitive wedge

02Beating Toro on the ground

Toro is the incumbent in the Grand Nord, and we respect what it has built. But our intelligence on Toro exposes a structural weakness we can turn into 360's opening.

Toro lives in daily life — present, available, woven into the routine. People may even pour it at night. But Toro does not actively go after the night. And the reason is structural: Toro sits inside the BRANA portfolio, which already owns the night through its other brands. Toro holds back from the night-time occasion on purpose — to avoid cannibalising its own stablemates.

That restraint is a door left open. 360 walks straight through it.

We do not attack Toro where it is strong — the day. We own where it will not go — the night — and then we earn the day through respect. It is a lane Toro cannot follow us into without breaking its own portfolio logic.

This is how 360 moves from a strong number two toward first place — not in one leap, but progressively: take the night Toro vacates, then out-respect Toro in the day it owns.

The audience

03Two pillars, one circle

We do not build the audience on demographics. We build it on a human truth, split into two pillars under one idea. One lives by day and drinks for the grind. One lives by night and drinks for the life. Together, they are 360 — the full circle of Haiti.

☀ Daily life · functional fuel

Pillar 1 — The Hard Workers

The backbone of Haiti. The masses. The informal economy that keeps the country standing. These are the people who choose, every single day, to carry their families on their backs.

  • The Madan Sara — the market women. Single mothers working twenty-hour days, moving goods across the country, feeding the nation, fighting for their children.
  • The men who carry the country — moto-taxi and taxi drivers, mécaniciens, ébénistes and the men who work the wood, constructeurs, agriculteurs — everyone who works under the sun and earns with their hands.

The insight: they do hard, honourable work — and that work demands real energy.

You chose to live at 360. You hustle to carry your family — you are a 360 supporter, a 360 man, a 360 woman. Everything you do, you do with honour, and honour takes energy. 360 is there for you.

Versus Toro: this is Toro's stronghold — its daily presence. We do not try to out-spend Toro here. We out-respect it — we honour the hustle in a way a portfolio brand never bothers to.

☾ Night life · experiential energy

Pillar 2 — The Young Generation

Young women and young men — hyped, ambitious, full of life. They want to build a life and live it.

  • What drives them — to study, to build a better future, to do everything with passion, to live boldly on their own terms.
  • What they refuse — a life dictated by anyone else. A life someone tells them how to live.

The insight: ambition and appetite for life both run on energy.

You need energy to study. Energy to build your future. Energy to advance. Energy to live a life nobody gets to dictate for you. So you do it at 360. 360 Live — live your life with passion, boldly.

Versus Toro: this is the open lane — the night Toro will not enter. Here, 360 is uncontested.

Owning the night

04The night occasion — and the mixer mechanic

We win the night through a series of 360 experiences: Meet & Greets, Speakeasy nights, and Speed Dating — Powered by 360, with Cherry as the hero flavour. These are 360-branded occasions where the brand lives inside the night as the energy of the room — never as a lecture.

A note on roles: 360 is the brand at the centre of everything — the events belong to 360. HaitiBrand is the agency that designs and directs the play, based on our analysis of this market and this competitor.

The in-venue mixer mechanic — explicit, but contained

  1. At a point in the night, 360 Cherry is served as a mixer — in the glass, with rum, and with vodka.
  2. It is not 360's own message telling people to mix. It runs through venue partnerships in the campaign cities — Cap-Haïtien, Les Gonaïves, Limonade. The bar owners and servers present it, and it appears on the menu — “360 Energy Cherry, mixed with rum / vodka” — without naming the rum or vodka brand.
  3. People already mix energy with spirits when they go out. We do not invent the behaviour — we organise and brand the room where it happens, so 360 becomes the energy of the night.
Responsible by design: the mixer association is kept out of 360's paid and social communication entirely. It lives only inside the venue and the menu — protecting the brand's responsible-marketing posture while 360 quietly owns the night-time occasion.
